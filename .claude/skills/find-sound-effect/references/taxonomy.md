# Content taxonomy (3rd Edition base game)

Condensed from the project design doc, for disambiguating which
`assets/audio/sfx/<category>/` a requested sound belongs in. Not
exhaustive on lore/flavor — just enough to route correctly and catch
spelling.

Landing, Chapel, Conservatory, Dining Room, Graveyard, Kitchen,
Laboratory, Larder, Laundry Chute, Gallery, Observatory, Statuary
Corridor, Tower, Catacombs, Chasm, Furnace Room, Panic Room, Ritual
Room, Secret Staircase, Underground Cavern, Underground Lake, Vault,
Armory, Gymnasium, Bloody Room, Charred Room, Collapsed Room, Guest
Quarters, Junk Room, Library, Primary Bedroom, Salon, Specimen Room,

## Heroes (`heroes/`)

Puleri, Sammy Angler, Jaden Jones, Isa Valencia, Anita Hernandez, Father
Warren Leung, Dan Nguyen M.D., Michelle Monroe, Brittani "Beat Box"

sting (`stings/`) over per-hero traitor stingers unless asked.

Shark, Security Robot, Bakeneko, Giant Hair Monster, Spirit, Head of the

Mummy, Skeleton, Ghoul, Revenant, Poltergeist, Banshee, Shadow Figure,
Possessed Doll, Demon, Witch, Gargoyle, Doppelganger, Chupacabra,
Yeti/Bigfoot-type, Masked Slasher, Chainsaw Maniac, Killer Clown,
Cultist, Plague Doctor, Grim Reaper, Giant Spider, Rat Swarm, Bat Swarm,
Locust/Insect Swarm, Hellhound, Sea Monster/Kraken,
Frankenstein's-Monster-type Creation, Scarecrow.

## Weapons (`weapons/`)
Only 5 in the 3rd-edition base game — don't add older-edition weapons
(Baseball Bat, Kitchen Knife, Shotgun, Axe, Rope, Spear, Sword) unless
the user says they're covering an expansion/older edition:

| Weapon | Suggested SFX |
|---|---|
| Machete | blade swing + slash impact |
| Chainsaw | rev-up + idle/revving loop + tearing impact |
| Gun | gunshot + shell-casing drop |
| Crossbow | bowstring release + bolt thud |
| Dynamite | fuse sizzle + explosion |

Plus weapon-agnostic combat one-shots (`stings/` or `weapons/`, your
call): unarmed punch/hit, miss/whiff, critical-hit sting,
character-down groan.

## Omens (9) and Events (`stings/`)
Omens: Armor, Book, Dagger, Dog, Holy Symbol, Idol, Mask, Ring, Skull —
each could get its own short card-draw chime, or share one generic
"Omen chime." Events: only 3 of 43 names are confirmed (Flickering
Lights, Wandering Ghost, Funeral) — for anything else, use 2-3 generic
Event stings ("eerie atmosphere," "sudden danger/trap," "beneficial/
lucky") rather than inventing card names.

## Other stings (`stings/`)
Room-entry stinger, Traitor reveal, Haunt-start sting.

## Music beds (`assets/audio/music/`)
3-5 looping tracks: exploration/ambient, tense/haunt-active, combat,
victory, defeat.
