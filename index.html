About the Sound Board

Betrayal Sound Board is a free, browser-based soundboard built for Betrayal at House on the Hill. Tap any tile to play an atmospheric sound effect for a monster, weapon, item, room, omen, or event, and layer a looping background score underneath to set the mood for your haunt. There's nothing to install — it runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or desktop.

Great for more than Betrayal

Though it's tuned for the game, these horror sound effects and ambient tracks work for any spooky evening:

  • Horror tabletop RPGs and board games — Dungeons & Dragons horror campaigns, Call of Cthulhu, and other haunt-style games
  • Halloween parties and haunted-house setups
  • Horror movie nights, escape rooms, and live scares
  • Atmosphere for streaming, storytelling, and creepy playlists

Frequently asked questions

Is the sound board free?

Yes — it's completely free, with no account, sign-up, or download required. Just open the page and play.

Do I need to own Betrayal at House on the Hill?

No. It's organized around the game's monsters, rooms, and events, but the sounds stand on their own for any horror atmosphere.

Can I use it for a Halloween party or a D&D night?

Absolutely. Trigger one-shot sound effects on demand and loop a background score for parties, tabletop RPGs, or horror movie nights.

Can I play music and sound effects at the same time?

Yes. The background score loops continuously while you tap individual sound-effect tiles over the top of it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — the board works in any modern mobile or desktop browser, no app needed.
